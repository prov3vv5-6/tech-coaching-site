Justin Moritz — Tech Coaching for Seniors

Hi, I’m Justin. I’m a Software Engineering graduate, but I’m not here to teach you to code. I’m here to help you get to the point where you don’t feel like throwing your phone out the window every time you use it. My goal is to help you use your phone, tablet, or computer with intentionality and stewardship — as a tool that serves your life, not one that adds stress to it.

I’ll meet you where you are, and I’ll never make you feel dumb for not knowing something. Together we can turn the parts of technology that feel scary, frustrating, or overwhelming into something manageable — maybe even enjoyable. The goal is simple: a digital life that’s organized and under control, so you can stop wrestling with your devices and get back to enjoying the real life in front of you.
