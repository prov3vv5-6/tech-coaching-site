Justin Moritz — Tech Coaching for Seniors

Hi, I’m Justin. I help people solve tech problems and to feel more confident using their phones, computers, and online accounts. We work step-by-step in a simple, patient, and clear way.

Instead of just fixing the issue and moving on, I explain what’s happening so you can handle common problems on your own in the future.

We can also organize and simplify your digital life—email, photos, files, apps, passwords, accounts, and calendars—so your technology feels manageable instead of stressful.

How It Works

  1. Click Book to choose a day and time that works for you
  2. On the booking page, you’ll be asked for your email and phone number, and whether you’d like a one-hour remote session or a one-hour in-person session
  3. Use the Pay button to confirm your booking
  4. You’ll receive a confirmation email after booking
  5. At your scheduled time, we solve your tech questions together, step-by-step

Choosing in person? In-person sessions are for friends and neighbors here in Twin Falls County, so the form will simply ask for your address.

Choosing remote? No problem — just leave your phone number and email, and I’ll reach out with easy, step-by-step instructions to get us connected.

After booking, please use the Pay button below to finalize your booking (pay button will open another window where you can pay for a $35 session with me by entering in your credit card information).
